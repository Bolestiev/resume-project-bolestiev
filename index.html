About me

I began my professional career as a migration lawyer, which provided me with a strong understanding of legal and social aspects of displacement and migration. Building on this foundation, I have accumulated over three years of experience in humanitarian and social research, with a primary focus on longitudinal studies, data collection, and qualitative analysis.
In July 2022, I joined Acted (REACH) as an Enumerator, and by September 2022, due to my strong performance and leadership skills, I was promoted to Team Leader. In this role, I managed and supervised field teams of 6 to 12 enumerators, ensuring high-quality and methodologically sound data collection.
Throughout my work with REACH and partner organizations, I participated in several large-scale projects, including MSNA (Multi-Sectoral Needs Assessment), IDP Councils, and HSM (Humanitarian Situation Monitoring). My responsibilities involved organizing and conducting interviews, focus group discussions, and remote assessments with displaced populations, returnees, and host communities.
My core area of expertise is longitudinal research, which allows me to monitor and analyze changes in humanitarian needs, migration trends, and community resilience over time. This long-term perspective has helped me provide valuable, evidence-based insights to strengthen humanitarian response efforts in Ukraine.
